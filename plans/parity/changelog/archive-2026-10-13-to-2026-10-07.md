- **Fix FileSystemFontProvider cross-fibre deadlock (2026-10-12)**: `FileSystemFontInfo#font` held a non-reentrant `Thread::Mutex` across `load_font`, which performs yielding font-file I/O. Under the cooperative scheduler an I/O stall in that section left the mutex held indefinitely while another fibre resolving the same font blocked on it (seen via `sample` as `FileSystemFontInfo#font → Thread::Mutex#lock`, reached from the rendering-suite and examples specs). Removed the per-info lock — Java's `synchronized` there only guards a benign double-load race and, unlike Crystal's `Thread::Mutex`, is reentrant — keeping the leaf-level `FontCache` lock.
